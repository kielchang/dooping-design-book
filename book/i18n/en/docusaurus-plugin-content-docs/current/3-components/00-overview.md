# Component Overview

## What belongs here

This chapter documents reusable interface primitives: their states, accessibility contract,
composition rules, and installation path. A component earns a page when it appears in more than
one workflow or protects a decision that teams repeatedly get wrong.

## What does not belong here

Product-specific screens, one-off wrappers, business calculations, and domain vocabulary belong
in the product or page documentation. Do not turn a component page into a second application
manual.

## Shared conventions

- Every component documents its default, hover, focus, disabled, loading, error, and empty states when applicable.
- Examples use real package exports and realistic data.
- Color is never the only cue; keyboard and screen-reader behavior are part of the API.
- Installation examples are copyable and include only the dependencies the component needs.
- Stories and tests are evidence of the contract, not decoration.
