# SegGroup

Small sets of mutually exclusive options that are visible and selectable at a glance.

<StoryLink id="元件-表單-輸入控制項--分段選擇" />

## When to use it and when not to

<Rules>
  <Do><p>Use it for <strong>2–5 options</strong> with short, fixed labels.</p></Do>
  <Dont><p>For more than five options, long labels, or options that change dynamically, use <a href="/components/checkbox-select">Select</a>. Once a segmented row wraps, it loses its “visible at a glance” advantage and becomes more confusing than a dropdown.</p></Dont>
</Rules>

## Accessibility is the point

- `role="radiogroup"` with each option as `role="radio"` and `aria-checked`.
- **Roving tabindex:** the group has only **one** Tab stop. Giving four options four stops makes keyboard users press Tab four times to leave one field.
- Arrow keys move between options; Space/Enter selects; Esc cancels.
- The selected option uses both a checkmark and a fill, not color alone.
