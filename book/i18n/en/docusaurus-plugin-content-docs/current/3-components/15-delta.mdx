# Delta

Shows how far a value differs from its baseline.

<StoryLink id="元件-狀態-變異・空狀態・分頁・步驟--變異顯示" />

## Triple encoding

**Arrow ▲▼ + text + color.** Color is the weakest of the three:

- About 8% of men have red–green color-vision deficiency.
- Admin reports are almost certainly printed and archived in grayscale.

Arrows and text must **make the statement on their own**; color only speeds recognition.

## `goodWhen`: good and bad are business decisions, not math

“Delta +100” can be good (more reserve) or bad (more accumulated error). The component does not guess; the caller explicitly says which direction is good.

<Rules>
  <Do><p>Every time you use Delta, ask: is a larger number good news or bad news for this user?</p></Do>
  <Dont><p>Do not assume “positive = green.” Positive cost, delay, and return rates are bad news.</p></Dont>
</Rules>

## Zero still needs to speak

`value === 0` renders “— unchanged,” not blank or `0`. Blank looks like missing data; `0` looks like a calculated result that may be wrong.
